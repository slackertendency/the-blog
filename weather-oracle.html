Weather Oracle 3000 is a weather data visualization built entirely in touch designer. local weather forecast data is fetched and visualized with the help of noise shaders. Parameters like average temperature, humidity, precipitation and wind speed are mapped to noise shaders creating distinct patterns indicative of intensity, amount and speed. The analogy of a crystal ball seemed fitting here because weather is often difficult to predict accurately even with modern day computation. Too many variables, constantly interacting with each other like the ingredients of a soup. Historically, pseudoscientific prediction models like astrology originated as an attempt to predict and understand weather. In many parts of the world, even today, knowing what the weather will be like is akin to knowing your own future. Good and timely rains lead to good crop yield and so on. Weather Lore The human brain seems to be attuned to spotting patterns. In many cultures, before the invention of devices like thermometers, lore around weather in the form of proverbs emerged as an attempt to pass on collective oral knowledge about the weather. These would often be informal and region specific. For example; When clouds look like black smoke A wise man will put on his cloak This study done by the meteorological department in the 1950s to examine regional Indian weather lore with meteorological data is a documentation of so much weather lore from different parts of the country. Process Through this project, I wanted to familiarize myself a bit more with DATs in Touch designer and work with data sets. I mainly just wanted to ‘see’ the weather and become more aware of my surroundings. In Goa, during the monsoon season specially it is good to be aware of when and how much will it rain! I also learnt how to use a weather API key to fetch data which was fun. Initially, I was requesting for daily weather forecasts but then I realized hourly data could be much more dynamic to look at. So, I added a call back to the API request to auto update the data every 10 seconds. As a non developer, so much of this was possible for me to do and understand because of ChatGPT. I also ended up using an old phone as a dedicated display for the orb so I could see it more often. I used OBS and video ninja.com to make this possible.
